Engineering method

RezMesh and BOUND Method v3.0

RezMesh is developed using BOUND Method v3.0 — Boundary-Oriented Unified Development. This page is a bridge to the methodology, not a duplicate of the canonical BOUND publication and website.

Why the relationship matters

RezMesh and BOUND are separate entities. RezMesh is the product; BOUND is the software engineering method used to structure its development.

Boundary-oriented engineering

BOUND concentrates architectural attention on responsibility boundaries and the contracts that cross them. The method is intended to make parallel implementation more independent while keeping shared behavior explicit.

Continuous verification

BOUND treats mechanically verifiable contracts and continuous conformance checking as part of the engineering system rather than as optional documentation.

Canonical methodology record

Detailed BOUND material belongs on the BOUND Method website and in its scholarly record. RezMesh links to those sources so the product site stays focused on the product itself.
