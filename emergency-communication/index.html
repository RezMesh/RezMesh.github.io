Emergency communication

Emergency Communication Without Internet

When ordinary communications are disrupted, the important question is not whether a cloud service is reachable. It is whether an urgent message can still move through the devices and people that remain nearby.

From one origin to a reachable local community

An emergency alert begins at one device. Directly reachable participants receive it first. Those devices can then help the information move farther through the available local mesh.

Propagation is geographic, not magical

The reachable area grows only where participating devices and viable local links exist. The website deliberately shows a finite coverage pattern rather than suggesting universal delivery.

Useful during disrupted local coordination

A shelter, medical point, volunteer group or family network may still need to exchange urgent local information when centralized communication is damaged, overloaded or unavailable.

Priority without exaggerated promises

RezMesh is designed to give emergency communication higher priority across the available mesh. Delivery still depends on the participating devices and paths that actually exist at the time.

RezMesh — RezMesh does not replace official emergency systems. It provides an infrastructure-independent local communication option when conventional connectivity cannot be relied upon.
