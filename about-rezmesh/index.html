About RezMesh

RezMesh is a resilient device-to-device communication project designed around situations where cellular service or internet infrastructure cannot be assumed.

RezMesh today

RezMesh is the current product name. The project may still appear in older references under the historical name RezvanMesh; those references describe the same project lineage.

Mission

The product begins from a simple premise: infrastructure can disappear while people and their devices remain. RezMesh explores how those devices can become local communication points.

Created by Soheil Mozaffari
