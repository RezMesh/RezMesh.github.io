Scenario · Outdoors

Rez-Mesh for Remote Hiking and Expeditions

Remote terrain makes the limitation obvious: a phone may still be useful even when the nearest tower is not. Rez-Mesh starts from the devices that the group already carries.

Beyond ordinary coverage

A hiking group can move into terrain where cellular coverage is absent or unreliable. Local communication therefore cannot assume that every message first reaches a carrier network.

A group can become a local path

Participating devices can discover nearby members and establish local communication relationships. When geometry permits, intermediate members can become part of a multi-device path.

Movement changes the topology

Hikers spread out, regroup and move around terrain. A route that was available a few minutes ago may change, making adaptation more important than a fixed connection model.

Battery matters in the field

Long trips make energy preservation part of communication planning. Rez-Mesh power states are designed to trade radio activity against longer device survival when appropriate.

Rez-Mesh — Stay connected beyond ordinary coverage—through participating nearby devices, not satellite-like range.
