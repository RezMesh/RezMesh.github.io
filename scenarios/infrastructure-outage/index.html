Scenario · Infrastructure outage

RezMesh During Infrastructure Outages

An outage can remove a central path without removing the devices at either end. RezMesh is designed to reorganize communication around the local devices that remain available.

The conventional path can fail centrally

A normal message may depend on a tower, carrier network, internet path and remote service even when sender and recipient are physically close.

Local communication changes the dependency

RezMesh allows participating devices to communicate locally rather than requiring every interaction to traverse distant infrastructure.

Outage and congestion are different problems with a similar effect

A network can be physically damaged, unavailable, blocked or simply overloaded. In each case, the user experiences the same practical problem: the normal path cannot be relied upon.

Continuity instead of spectacle

The product story is intentionally calm. The important visual is not infrastructure failing dramatically; it is communication continuing after the failure.

RezMesh — Communication that does not begin with a cloud connection.
