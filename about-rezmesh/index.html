About Rez-Mesh

Rez-Mesh is a resilient device-to-device communication project designed around situations where cellular service or internet infrastructure cannot be assumed.

Rez-Mesh today

Rez-Mesh is the current product name. The project may still appear in older references under the historical name RezvanMesh; those references describe the same project lineage.

Mission

The product begins from a simple premise: infrastructure can disappear while people and their devices remain. Rez-Mesh explores how those devices can become local communication points.
